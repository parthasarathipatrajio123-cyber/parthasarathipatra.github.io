Who I am

About Me

🎓

Background

I am a PhD research fellow at the Department of English, Jadavpur University, working on South Asian Literature. I am a first-generation learner and a person with disabilities born with cerebral palsy. Having grown up in the under-privileged town of Bankura, I have faced innumerable challenges on my journey to academia.

✊

Mission

My academic and personal journey is driven by a commitment to humanities research, disability rights advocacy, and understanding the geopolitical history of South Asia. I believe that through scholarship and social engagement, we can reshape how society understands and includes persons with disabilities.

"I can't change the way I look, but I can aspire to change the way the world looks at me."

Skills & Interests

Humanities Research & Teaching Creative & Communication Skills Disability & Minority Rights Advocacy Geopolitical History of South Asia Editorial & Digital Content Creation Bengali (Mother Tongue) English (Fluent) Hindi (Basic)

Services

📚

Academic & Research Consultation

One-on-one as well as group mentorship in communicative English, Literature and Humanities Studies for High School, Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students, alongside guidance and consultation for research in South Asian Literature, postcolonial theory, trauma studies, necropolitics, Kashmiri narratives, disability studies, and the geopolitical history of South Asia. Also teaching TCESS topics to STEM students at Jadavpur University since 2023.

✍

Content Development & Digital Writing

Writing and editing academic and business content, copy editing, proofreading, presentation preparation, and related services for blogs, newsletters, digital platforms, and editorial projects with a focus on humanities, social issues, and disability rights narratives, backed by a Post-Graduate Certificate in Editing and Publishing from SCTR, Jadavpur University.

🌐

Translation (Bengali ↔ English)

Translation of academic and literary texts between Bengali and English. Certified with a UGC-approved Certificate Course on Translation as a Skill from CENTIL, Jadavpur University (2022).

✊

Disability Rights Advocacy

Awareness sessions, panel discussions, consultations, and advocacy support for persons with disabilities in academic and institutional settings, drawing on lived experience and CP Rise Leadership training by EnAble India.

🎤

Business Communication and Impactful Storytelling

Helping individuals and institutions communicate with clarity and purpose — presentations, pitches, institutional narratives, and personal or organizational storytelling that connects with an audience, drawing on lived experience turning a personal story into public advocacy and research into accessible writing.

📈

Securities Market Investment and Portfolio Analysis

Accessible, research-driven equity market insights and portfolio guidance for retail investors — reviewing existing holdings, screening stocks and sectors, and explaining market trends and risk in plain language, to help individuals make informed, confident investment decisions in the Indian equity market.
