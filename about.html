October 2026 Disability Rights & Mobility

My Second Adapted Scooter — A Long Overdue Win

I am happy to share that my second adapted scooter purchase has finally happened, after a long delay just before the Durgapujo! 🚲 I'm sharing the whole story in case it helps anyone else with a locomotor disability in their purchase of adapted scooter.

As a person with cerebral palsy, an adapted scooter is my everyday means of mobility and independent living. My first one came from Glorious Honda, Bankura town branch in 2014. Back then the paperwork was all manual. I got concessions on road tax, and a driving licence in the Invalid Carriage category.

Since 2022 I've been living between Kolkata and Bankura, and I needed a second scooter. Everyday commutation turned out to be harder than I expected. Honda showrooms in South Kolkata, and even Glorious Honda in Bankura, declined to sell me a scooter under the Divyangjan category — they weren't sure of the government rules and procedures, or maybe didn't want the extra paperwork. So I went and found out myself, first at RTO Bankura, then at Paribahan Bhawan and GST Bhawan in Kolkata.

Around the same time, through EnAble India's CP Rise Leadership Development Programme, I did a project on the challenges adapted scooter users face. Almost everyone I spoke to, in both Kolkata and Bankura, had similar experiences — dealers who didn't register scooters as Divyangjan vehicles, leaving users without tax concessions or the right category of driving licence, despite valid disability certificates.

After many appeals, the Glorious Honda, Bankura dealership finally agreed, worked it out with the Bankura Sadar RTO officials, and the scooter came home just before the Pujo. A big thank you to the Glorious Honda, Bankura Town dealership and the RTO Bankura Sadar officials.

To everyone with locomotor disabilities who wishes to purchase an adapted scooter: the rules exist, so don't hesitate to fight until you reclaim your rights! Now pray for my safety while I enjoy every joyride! 😊
